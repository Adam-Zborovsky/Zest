# M8 independent review — Accounts and synced collection

**Date:** 2026-09-14
**Reviewers:** Fresh Sonnet 5 subagent (round 1, read-only, no part in building M8); fresh Sonnet 5 subagent (round 2, read-only); integrator (after round 2).
**Scope:** Round 1 commits `0eb11ee..ad3339c`. Round 2 scope: the fix commits. Integrator scope: test fake against backend and integration points.
**Verdict:** APPROVE WITH FIXES. All M8 acceptance criteria were met after fixes. Round 1 raised one blocker, three major findings, two minor findings, and two notes; all were resolved. Round 2 raised one major finding; resolved. Integrator identified two major and two minor findings; all resolved.

## Round 1 findings and resolutions

| # | Severity | Finding | Resolution |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Blocker | Drift stored timestamps as whole seconds. A second edit to the same entry within the same second was silently lost under last-edit-wins. | Fixed in `6a27f51`. Timestamps now stored in milliseconds. Every local write sets `max(now, previous + 1ms)` to strictly advance even on repeated clock reads or backwards clocks. |
| 2 | Major | The sign-out push bypassed the single-flight guard and could race an in-flight pass. | Fixed in `cdc0542`. Sign-out push now guarded by the same `_syncing` lock as other sync passes. |
| 3 | Minor | Several `is SyncEngine` casts after branching on the sync provider type. | Fixed in `632d1fe`. `CollectionSync` interface widened to cover all needed methods; casts removed. |
| 4 | Minor | A redundant photo size check after `bodyLimit` in the backend. | Fixed in `31ed584` with a defense-in-depth comment explaining the rationale. |
| 5 | Note | No `/auth/me` check at launch, by design. | Confirmed as intentional; the app loads the stored account from `shared_preferences` and token from `flutter_secure_storage` at startup, so launch is synchronous and no splash state exists. |
| 6 | Note | Precision gap (whole seconds vs. milliseconds) was undocumented. | Fixed in `6a27f51` with a documented rule: timestamps are stored as UTC milliseconds; every write strictly advances the timestamp. |

## Round 2 findings and resolutions

| # | Severity | Finding | Resolution |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Major | The push applied the server's echo of an equal-timestamp no-op unconditionally. A tied pending edit was lost on its next push, because the response with `updatedAt` equal to the sent timestamp was treated as success instead of rejection. | Fixed in `c57a335`. A push whose returned record ties the sent `updatedAt` (or `photoUpdatedAt`) but carries different content is now a server-side rejection: the client leaves it dirty, re-stamps its local `updatedAt` strictly past what was sent, and retries once in the same pass. Returned tombstones are always adopted. The photo path got the same fix. Clocks running backwards are unbounded; concurrent edits resolve deterministically but not necessarily in human order. |

## Integrator findings and resolutions

| # | Severity | Finding | Resolution |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Major | The Flutter test fake server diverged from the real server. The real server keeps its own `hasPhoto`, `photoUpdatedAt`, and `createdAt` on an entry PUT, and stamps `photoUpdatedAt` when clearing a photo. The fake did neither. The new tie check would have misfired on every photo change against the real server. | Fixed in `7c33987`. The tie check now compares only client-owned fields. The fake mirrors `backend/src/accounts/repository.ts` and has its own tests. |
| 2 | Major | Applying a server record deleted a pending local photo before it was pushed, losing a newly added photo. | Fixed in `7c33987`. Pending photos now survive except on photo push success or a tombstone. |
| 3 | Minor | The Postgres 18 Compose volume must mount at `/var/lib/postgresql`. | Fixed in `c91ae0d`. Adam's rename to `backend/docker-compose.dev.yml` is kept. |
| 4 | Minor | The profile sheet said "Signed in since" but showed the account creation date. | Fixed in `e35e68e`. Label changed to "Account since". |
