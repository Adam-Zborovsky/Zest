# M8 independent review — Accounts and synced collection

**Date:** 2026-09-14
**Reviewers:** a fresh Sonnet 5 subagent (round 1, read-only, no part in building M8); a second fresh Sonnet 5 subagent (round 2, read-only); the integrator (after round 2).
**Scope:** Round 1 covered commits `0eb11ee..ad3339c`. Round 2 covered the round 1 fix commits `6a27f51..31ed584`. The integrator compared the Flutter test fake with the backend and checked the integration points.
**Verdict:** Round 1 was BLOCK. Round 2 was APPROVE WITH FIXES. Every finding from both rounds and from the integrator is resolved.
- **Round 1:** one blocker, one major, two minor, and two notes.
- **Round 2:** one major and one note.
- **Integrator:** two major and two minor.

No mismatches were found between the Flutter HTTP clients and the backend routes.

## Round 1 findings and resolutions

| # | Severity | Finding | Resolution |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Blocker | Drift stored timestamps as whole seconds. A second edit to the same entry within the same second tied, and the server treated the tie as no change. The client then applied the stale server record and cleared its dirty flag, so the edit was silently lost. | Fixed in `6a27f51`. Collection timestamps are stored as integer milliseconds, with a schema 4 migration. Every local write sets `max(now, previous + 1ms)`. |
| 2 | Major | The sign-out push bypassed the single-flight guard and could race an in-flight pass while the token was being cleared. | Fixed in `cdc0542`. Sign-out cancels the pending debounce timer, waits for any running pass, then runs the final push under the same `_inFlight` guard. |
| 3 | Minor | `is SyncEngine` casts in `session_controller.dart` and `collection_providers.dart`. | Fixed in `632d1fe`. `CollectionSync` gained `scheduleAfterLocalWrite` and `pushBeforeSignOut`, and both casts are removed. |
| 4 | Minor | A redundant photo size check after `bodyLimit` in the backend. | Kept as defense in depth, with a comment explaining why (`31ed584`). |
| 5 | Note | The app does not call `/auth/me` at launch, so a session the server has already revoked is only discovered on the first sync. | Intentional per `docs/ACCOUNTS.md`: a 401 on any authenticated call expires the session. |
| 6 | Note | The timestamp precision rule was undocumented. | Documented in `docs/ACCOUNTS.md` § Client behavior (`6a27f51`). |

## Round 2 findings and resolutions

| # | Severity | Finding | Resolution |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Major | The push applied the server's echo of an equal-timestamp no-op without checking it. A pending edit that tied on timestamp got the server's record back, and the dirty flag was cleared, losing the edit. | Fixed in `c57a335`. A returned record that ties the sent timestamp but carries different content counts as a rejected tie. The row stays dirty, is re-stamped 1 ms later, and is retried once in the same pass. Returned tombstones are always adopted. The photo path got the same fix. |
| 2 | Note | Nothing bounds a clock that runs backwards. | Documented in `docs/ACCOUNTS.md`. Concurrent edits from devices with wrong clocks resolve deterministically, but not necessarily in the order people made them. |

## Integrator findings and resolutions

| # | Severity | Finding | Resolution |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Major | The Flutter test fake diverged from the real server. On an entry PUT, the real `upsertEntry` keeps its own `hasPhoto`, `photoUpdatedAt`, and `createdAt`, and `clearEntryPhoto` stamps `photoUpdatedAt`. The fake echoed the client's values instead. Against the real server, the new full-content tie check would have misread every photo change as a rejected tie. | Fixed in `7c33987`. The tie check compares only client-owned fields. The fake now mirrors `backend/src/accounts/repository.ts` and has its own tests. |
| 2 | Major | Applying a server record deleted a pending local photo before it was pushed, losing a newly added photo in the same pass. | Fixed in `7c33987`. A pending photo survives unless the photo push itself succeeds or the entry is tombstoned. |
| 3 | Minor | The Postgres 18 Compose volume must mount at `/var/lib/postgresql`; the old `/data` path makes the container fail to start. | Fixed in `c91ae0d`, keeping Adam's rename to `backend/docker-compose.dev.yml`. |
| 4 | Minor | The profile sheet said "Signed in since" but showed the account creation date. | Fixed in `e35e68e`: "Account since". |

## Not verified

- Real Docker Postgres; the tests use PGlite.
- The app running against the real server end to end.
- On-device `flutter_secure_storage` and LAN cleartext traffic.
- Drift `onUpgrade` transactionality during a crash.
- A narrow timing gap between the debounce timer and sign-out; this was reasoned through, not reproduced.
- Photo tie identity, which is judged by `hasPhoto` rather than by comparing bytes.
- A physical screen reader.
